I am a Senior Research Fellow at the Centre for Education Policy and Equalising Opportunities (CEPEO), UCL, where I study inequality in education and the labour market, and how climate change and the transition to net zero will reshape it, using applied econometrics and linked administrative data.

My recent work focuses on higher education: the role of teacher assessments and predicted grades in university admissions, the causes and consequences of inequalities in university mismatch, and the returns to a university degree. I am also an affiliated researcher at the Department for Education through the Economics of Education Research Centre (EERC).

My climate work will begin with the effects of extreme weather on school attendance.

I received my PhD in economics from Sciences Po in 2022, supervised by Ghazala Azmat and Jean-Marc Robin.
